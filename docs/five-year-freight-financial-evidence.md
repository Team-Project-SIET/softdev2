# Five-year native financial evidence

OpenTTD 15.3 [company API](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/blob/15.3/src/script/api/script_company.hpp) and [implementation](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/blob/15.3/src/script/api/script_company.cpp) define current quarter index 0 and previous completed quarter index 1. Preserve sampled quarter history before it ages out. Income measures transport receipts. [Company accounting](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/blob/15.3/src/company_cmd.cpp), `SubtractMoneyFromAnyCompany`, records signed quarterly expenses only for vehicle running costs, property and loan interest. Construction, new vehicles and OTHER entries do not enter this quarterly expense aggregate. Category splitting is unavailable through the GameScript read API.

Native observation rejects company autorenew being enabled (`GetAutoRenewStatus(0)`), preserving the no automatic vehicle purchases policy.

The continuous observer captures company cash, loan principal, current/previous quarterly income, expense aggregate and delivered cargo. It captures per-vehicle current/previous economy-year profit, annual running-cost rate, loaded cargo, capacity and station waiting cargo. The running-cost rate is not actual spending. Profit is not revenue. Capacity utilization is an instantaneous sample, not a journey average. Delivery monitor reads reset their accumulator; the bridge accumulates each verified delta. A service cargo total is available only where its destination-industry/commodity monitor belongs exclusively to that service. Shared pairs remain UNKNOWN. Polling cannot count all completed delivery cycles, so those counts remain UNKNOWN. Concurrent service revenue remains UNKNOWN; aggregate company freight income is measured.

Cash reconciliation separately records transport revenue, command-verified CAPEX, loan-principal changes, signed expense aggregate and residual unidentified movements. Vehicle OPEX, loan interest, property/OTHER category splits, operating profit, ROI and net break-even remain UNKNOWN unless independently measured. Gross revenue divided by CAPEX is explicitly a gross ratio, not profitability. The operating-window opening cash is captured after all three services are ready; construction CAPEX is retained separately and must not be deducted a second time from this window.
